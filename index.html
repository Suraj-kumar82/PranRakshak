How PRANरक्षक Saves Lives

A seamless, three-step bridge between unconscious patients, first responders, and emergency trauma centers.

1

1. Create Medical Profile

Enter your blood group, life-threatening drug allergies, ongoing prescriptions, and emergency contacts into your encrypted vault.

2

2. Carry Smart QR Card

Generate your physical wallet card, helmet sticker, or phone lock-screen QR. No sensitive passwords or full medical records are exposed.

3

3. Responders Scan & Act

Any unknown helper or paramedic scans the card with any standard phone camera. Zero login required to dial family or dispatch 112 with GPS.

Engineered for the Golden Hour

Critical Emergency Capabilities

Built with healthcare architects to tackle India's most urgent emergency response gaps.

Critical Allergy Warnings

Prevents fatal medication errors. Paramedics immediately see anaphylactic alerts like Penicillin, Aspirin, or Sulfa before administering drugs.

1-Tap Emergency Calling

Bystanders can call the patient's spouse or family with a single tap, plus auto-generated WhatsApp/SMS location broadcasts.

High-Accuracy Geolocation

Auto-detects GPS coordinates of the accident scene so callers can read aloud exact latitude/longitude coordinates to the 112 operator.

Hospital & Fleet Dispatch

Connects civilian emergency requests directly to hospital triage desks, tracking Advanced Life Support (ALS) ambulances with live ETAs.

Printable Wallet Card

High-resolution export to print wallet-sized emergency ID cards or helmet stickers that work even without cell coverage.

Privacy-First Architecture

Only life-saving emergency indicators are exposed to the public QR view. Routine private records remain securely protected.
